**SoulSync 3.6.7 is out** :musical_note:

The Settings page is rebuilt in React and saves only what you changed.

Security: only the admin can make the first API key now, and members can only do what their profile allows. Worth updating soon.

SoulSync can now answer as a Seerr server, so Litefin and other Seerr apps can discover and request movies and shows through the video side (thanks Thibs, #2151). Turn it on under Settings, System, SoulSync API.

Server Activity is rebuilt for Plex and Jellyfin: what is playing and why it transcodes, stop with a message, and SoulSync's own play history and stats.

Albums get an Other versions picker with a preview before it switches (thanks wolf39us, #2145). Popular tracks get a flame on the album page (#2144), the artist page shows which albums a rename would move (#2141), and locking an album keeps you on the artist page (#2143).

Band names with an "&" like Above & Beyond now stay one artist in tags, credits and folders, and Group Therapy style numbered releases no longer cross (thanks SeadogsBooty, #2131, #2133 to #2136). Release types go by length too, so Wish You Were Here is an album again.

For request users, video posters show where a title stands, Discover always loads with a cleaner layout, members land on a page they can open, and admins can set and lock what each profile shows (thanks SeadogsBooty, #2154 to #2164).

Thanks to nstrelow (#2140), PapaBearDoes (#2152) and ginevra-70 (#2115) for their contributions.

Also: Jellyfin playlists are private (#2148), Apple Music mirrors refresh again (#2147), artists keep their folder when a source renames them (#2165), artist.nfo lands in the artist folder with poster and background art (#2138), guest artists no longer fill the Library list (#2142), Asked for and Got are the right way round in review, and a video import folder (#2129).

Full notes on GitHub.
