**SoulSync 3.7.0 is out** :musical_note:

**SoulSync now has its own player.** Watch and listen to your movies, TV, music, YouTube, audiobooks, podcasts and live guide, straight from your SoulSync server, like a Plex or Jellyfin player. Open it with **Open the player** at the top of the sidebar. The first time, an admin runs a short setup: name the server, sort folders into libraries (4K, Kids, Anime) and choose who sees what. Every profile arranges its own rows.

**The app.** The player on Android phones, tablets and TV, with offline downloads. Pair it from Settings, Devices.
Repo: <https://github.com/Nezreka/soulsync-mobile-app>
APK: <https://github.com/Nezreka/soulsync-mobile-app/releases/download/v0.2.0/SoulSync-Player-0.2.0.apk>

**Chapters, skip intro, parties and casting.** Skip intro when the file marks one, watch parties with a countdown and reactions, and Chromecast that plays anything.

**Video without a media server.** SoulSync Standalone scans your movie and TV folders itself, and Plex and Jellyfin can run as one library.

**Backups.** One Backups page, every database covered, a restore that checks first, and a repair for a damaged database. "Keep 5" keeping only one is fixed.

**Requests and people.** A playlist's songs are one request (#2302), members can ask for a feature (#2304), and a member no longer sees the server's connected playlists (#2301).

**Playlists.** Search public playlists by name (#2305), sync several at once with or without downloading (#2309), and songs only YouTube has download from their own video (#2306).

**Search and activity.** Identify a song by listening (thanks Jygijiji). Player streams show in Server Activity and can be stopped.

**Downloads.** Four ways a download could lose a file are closed, tribute acts are refused, and deep verify can catch fake lossless (off by default).
