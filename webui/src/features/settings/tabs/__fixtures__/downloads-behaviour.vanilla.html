Download Behaviour

Preview source, concurrency & timeouts
i
Where to stream audio when previewing tracks. YouTube is instant and requires no setup. "Active Download Source" uses your configured source (Tidal, Qobuz, etc.) — if that's Soulseek, YouTube is used automatically. "Deezer 30s Preview" plays Deezer's 30 second clip of the song straight away, no Deezer account needed. Songs Deezer has no clip for use YouTube. With queue auto-download off, missing queue songs play their clip instead of being skipped.
i
Maximum simultaneous downloads per batch. Soulseek album downloads always use 1 worker due to per-user upload limits. Higher values speed up large playlists and wishlists. Takes effect after a restart, since the worker threads are created at startup.
i
How long to wait on a streaming source's API during search (HiFi, Qobuz, Deezer, and the stream preview search). 0 (or blank) = each source's built-in default — HiFi/Qobuz 15s, Deezer 10s. Raise it if a slow connection makes searches give up too early; lower means failing over to the next source faster. Clamped to 5–120. Soulseek is not affected — it has its own Search Timeout setting above.
Checks every selected source & configured connection.
