MetaSync

Join SIN, the signal network, through the MetaSync companion
MetaSync is a small companion program that runs next to SoulSync and joins pools for you — the first pool is SIN, the signal network from step 2 below. Two separate steps happen on this page, and they are not the same thing:
Step 1 connects SoulSync and MetaSync to each other, on this machine.
Step 2 joins a pool. MetaSync does the joining — SoulSync itself never talks to a pool, it only hands MetaSync your pool key.
Installing MetaSync joins nothing by itself.
Connecting pairs the two programs. When you click Connect, a code appears here and in MetaSync's dashboard; approve it there and the two programs swap keys automatically — MetaSync gets a key to read this SoulSync, SoulSync gets a key to talk to MetaSync. You never type or see either key.

Rather type a key? Connect manually instead of pairing
Pairing above does this for you. The manual way: in MetaSync's dashboard, create a local API key and paste it here, then Save. SoulSync ends up holding the same kind of key either way.
A pool is a network your MetaSync can join. The first pool is SIN, the SoulSync Intelligence Network: it collects anonymous taste signals from SoulSync installs — bulk counts of what people watchlist and wishlist, never your personal data — and turns them into shared playlists, like Trending and Most Wanted, which MetaSync brings back into this SoulSync. Get a key from your pool account, put it below, and send it to MetaSync. MetaSync joins and does all the talking — it swaps your key for short-lived access tokens, so the key itself stays between you and MetaSync. Nothing is joined until you send the key.
No key yet? Get one at the SIN front desk — sign in there, create a key, then come back and put it below.
