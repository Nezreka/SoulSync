Free lossless downloads via community-run hifi-api instances. No authentication or subscription needed.
Heads up: some HiFi instances only have 30-second Tidal previews for certain tracks, with headers faked to look like the full song. SoulSync decodes every HiFi download to catch these and never lets a shorter clip replace a good library file, but if a track just won't come through at full length here, grab it from another source.
