what was going on: picking a version only moved the release match. when you picked Country Grammar (17 tracks) on the second album, the match moved off your Country Grammar album onto the (Hot S**t) one. nothing ever merged the two rows, so the files stayed split. the album row also kept the old title, type and date from the 5 track (Hot S**t) single it was first matched to. that's why the picker said Country Grammar was current while the row still said (Hot S**t), dated 2000-02-29, in the EP section.

what changed:
- picking a version now puts that version's title, type, date and track count on the album. a 17 track album goes back under Albums. a type you set by hand still wins
- when another album of the same artist already is that version, or has the same name and no match of its own, the preview offers "Merge with ..." next to the switch button. merging makes them one album. a song both have becomes one track with both files, the rest move over. no files are deleted
- the current version has a Preview button now too, so you can fix or merge from either album
- a deluxe next to a standard edition is never offered as a merge, and a locked album isn't merged away

once you're on the update:
1. open (Hot S**t) Country Grammar, then Other versions
2. Preview on Country Grammar (17 tracks, the one marked current)
3. hit "Merge with Country Grammar" and confirm
4. if the merge button isn't there, do the same from Country Grammar's own Other versions: Preview the 17 track one and it'll offer the merge from that side
5. on the album that's left, run Rename / Organize so the files end up in one folder. if any song was in both folders it'll show 2 files on that track, and you can keep the better one

if anything still looks off after that let me know.
